🔍 SEO Quick Setup

3 steps to get Ligerwave indexed and measured. About 1.5 hours total. Tick each box as you go — progress saves automatically in your browser.
1

Google Search Console

What it does: Tells Google your site exists. Shows you which keywords people search to find you, how many clicks you get, and whether Google can crawl your pages properly.

Time: ~30 minutes

Cost: Free

Steps:

  • Go to search.google.com/search-console
  • Sign in with your Google account (Gmail)
  • Click "Add property" → choose "Domain" (not URL prefix)
  • Enter: ligerwave.tech
  • Click "Continue"
  • Copy the TXT record value Google gives you
  • Log into Cloudflare dashboard → select ligerwave.tech → DNS → Add record
  • Type: TXT, Name: @, Value: paste what Google gave you
  • Click Save, then back in Search Console click "Verify"
  • Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" in the left menu
  • Enter: sitemap.xml and click Submit
  • After 24-48 hours, check the "Performance" tab to see your first search data
📎 https://search.google.com/search-console
📎 https://dash.cloudflare.com

3

Lighthouse Audit

What it does: Tests your site speed, accessibility, SEO, and best practices — and gives you a score out of 100. Investors and customers judge your company by this number. A score above 90 signals quality.

Time: ~30 minutes

Cost: Free — built into Chrome

Steps:

  • Open Google Chrome on your computer
  • Go to https://ligerwave.tech
  • Press F12 (or right-click anywhere → Inspect)
  • Click the "Lighthouse" tab (may be in the ">>" menu)
  • Check these boxes:
    ☑ Performance
    ☑ Accessibility
    ☑ Best Practices
    ☑ SEO
  • Device: "Mobile" (important — test on mobile, not desktop)
  • Click "Analyze page load"
  • Wait ~30 seconds for the report
  • Take a screenshot of the score card for your pitch deck
  • If any score is below 90, message me the specific issues and I'll fix them

Target scores for VC-ready:

  • Performance: >90 (fast loading)
  • Accessibility: >95 (works for everyone)
  • Best Practices: >95 (secure and modern)
  • SEO: >95 (Google-friendly)
