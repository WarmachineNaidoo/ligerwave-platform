🛡️ Chargeback Defence Protocol

Every transaction records: customer IP, device fingerprint, CSI session ID (if active), consent audit trail, email verification, payment gateway transaction ID, and 3DS authentication status. This data is retained for 5 years to dispute Section 75 / chargeback claims.

Paystack integration: Webhook POST /payments/webhook receives chargeback notifications automatically. Fraud scoring via Paystack's KYC + our CSI behavioural analysis (does the paying user's walking gait match their account's known gait?).
