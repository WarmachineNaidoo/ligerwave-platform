A Wall Doesn't Stop a Person

EstateGuard • Perimeter monitoring that works through walls
June 2026 • 4 min read

Dainfern Estate spent R12 million on perimeter security. Two-metre walls. Electric fencing. CCTV cameras along the entire boundary. The intruder climbed a tree, dropped over the wall, and was inside the property for 11 minutes before the control room noticed. He entered three homes and was gone before the armed response vehicle arrived.

The fence worked. It detected the climb. An alert was generated. But the guard at the monitor had 30 other camera feeds to watch. By the time he located the alert, the intruder was already between two houses — invisible to every camera along the perimeter.

"R12 million on perimeter security. The intruder climbed a tree. Nobody saw him for 11 minutes."

EstateGuard doesn't replace the fence or the cameras. It adds a third layer: continuous CSI-based monitoring that tracks every person inside the perimeter — not just at the wall, but between the houses, through the walls, in real time. It distinguishes a person from a vehicle. It knows an intruder from a resident by gait signature. It shows the guard exactly where every person is on the estate, updating every two seconds.

A wall stops someone for a few seconds. EstateGuard tracks them for the entire time they're inside.
