The Silent Deck

MaritimeGuard • Man-overboard detection for the shipping industry
June 2026 • 4 min read

A crew member on the Safmarine SA Express was last seen on deck at 02:00 during his watch. The next scheduled check at 04:00 noted his absence. By the time the ship turned back to search the area, 45 minutes had elapsed. The body was never recovered.

The investigation concluded: "Presumed man overboard. Time of incident unknown. Circumstances unknown."

"Presumed man overboard, time of incident unknown" — Safmarine SA Express incident report, 2019

Maritime regulations require man-overboard drills, lifebuoys, and recovery procedures. But every system relies on someone witnessing the incident. In the dark, in rough weather, on a busy deck — nobody witnesses most maritime falls.

MaritimeGuard uses the same CSI technology as our construction and prison products, adapted for shipboard environments. It monitors open deck areas, accommodation corridors, and helideck perimeters. When a person who was present one second is absent the next — at the rail, near an open hatch, during a watch change — the system alerts the bridge within seconds. Not 45 minutes. Seconds. In cold water, that difference is measured in lives saved.
