He Died in His Sleep, Alone

Wellness+ • Non-contact breathing and heart rate monitoring
June 2026 • 4 min read

An elderly resident at a care home in Protea Glen, Soweto, passed away in his sleep. Staff discovered him at the 06:00 check-in. He had been gone for six hours.

The facility had invested in safety technology. Motion sensors in every room. Pressure mats on every bed. Emergency pull cords within reach. None of these devices detected the one event that mattered: a person who simply stopped breathing.

"He had a motion sensor, a pressure mat, and an emergency cord. None of them detected that he had stopped breathing."

Motion sensors detect movement. Pressure mats detect weight. Emergency cords detect intentional action. None of them detect the absence of life. Wellness+ was built to fill that gap — using the same WiFi CSI technology that powers our security products to measure breathing rate, heart rate, and sleep patterns without any contact or wearable device.

Wellness+ is not a medical device. It is not certified for clinical diagnosis. But it detects when someone stops breathing — and alerts someone who can help. In a care home with 30 residents, one WiFi access point covers every room. No wearables. No batteries. No training required.

Six hours is too long. Wellness+ reduces that gap to seconds.
