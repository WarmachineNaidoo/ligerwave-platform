Six Seconds

ConstructionGuard • Fall detection, crane proximity, trench safety
June 2026 • 4 min read

A section of bridge decking under construction on Johannesburg's M1 highway collapsed without warning. Two workers fell 12 metres. Neither survived. The investigation found vibration monitoring equipment was on-site but nobody was watching the readings when the critical threshold was crossed.

Falls from height are the leading cause of death on South African construction sites. The OHS Act requires harnesses, guardrails, and safety nets — yet workers still fall. The problem isn't a lack of safety equipment. It's that nobody knows the moment it happens.

"The harness was intact. The anchor point was secure. The worker fell anyway. Nobody saw it."

ConstructionGuard detects a fall within one second — not by watching the worker, but by measuring the change in radio signals as a body moves vertically through space. It distinguishes a fall from normal movement, confirms via heart rate, and alerts the site safety officer immediately. It also monitors crane blind spots, trench wall vibration, and confined space entries — all through the same CSI sensors, all without requiring the worker to carry or wear anything.
