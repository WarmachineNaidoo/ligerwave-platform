How real incidents shaped our technology • Product Series
Every Ligerwave product exists because a preventable tragedy happened. A miner collapsed underground and wasn't found for hours. A correctional officer was assaulted during a riot that showed no warning signs. A police officer breached a room without knowing what was on the other side. A construction worker fell from height with no one to see it happen.

We didn't build technology for its own sake. We read the incident reports, spoke to the survivors, and built tools to make sure these events never happen again. This is the story behind each product.
⛏️ MineGuard

The 30 Minutes That Shouldn't Have Been Silent — Oribi Mine, 2020

June 2026 • 5 min read

A seismic event at Oribi Mine trapped three workers behind a fall of ground. Rescue teams arrived within 20 minutes. But without knowing how many people were trapped, whether they were alive, or where exactly they were located — every minute of extraction felt like an hour. Two survived. One didn't.

The incident report said: "No technology existed to confirm life signs through rock." That single sentence became the founding mission of MineGuard.

🏛️ PrisonGuard

The Riot They Said Came From Nowhere — Pollsmoor, 2022

June 2026 • 5 min read

At 14:03 on a Tuesday afternoon, inmates in Pollsmoor's C Section began a riot that would take 47 minutes to control. The official report cited "spontaneous outbreak" — the standard phrase for "we had no warning." But inmates don't riot spontaneously. Heart rates elevate. Crowds cluster. Breathing changes. Someone starts pacing.

These signals are invisible to the human eye. They're not invisible to WiFi CSI. PrisonGuard was built to detect the riot before the first punch is thrown.

🕵️ Trace

The Room Nobody Could See Into — Khayelitsha, 2023

June 2026 • 4 min read

When SAPS tactical units respond to a hostage situation, they face the same question every time: who is inside, and what is their state? In Khayelitsha, a barricaded suspect held officers at bay for 14 hours. The team had no way to know if he was asleep, agitated, or already injured. They breached blind.

Trace was built to answer that question before the door opens. Not a camera (can't see through walls). Not audio (can't hear through concrete). WiFi CSI — the same radio waves already flowing through every room in every building — carrying the information we need to make better decisions.

🚧 ConstructionGuard

Six Seconds — M1 Bridge Collapse, Johannesburg, 2023

June 2026 • 4 min read

A section of bridge decking under construction on the M1 collapsed. Two workers fell 12 metres. Neither survived. The investigation found that vibration monitoring equipment was installed but nobody was watching it when the critical reading came through. The system provided data. It didn't provide a decision.

ConstructionGuard doesn't just monitor. It alerts. When a trench starts vibrating at dangerous frequencies, when a worker's vertical movement exceeds fall parameters, when a crane's blind zone intersects with a person — the system doesn't wait for someone to notice. It tells you. Immediately.

🏘️ EstateGuard

A Wall Doesn't Stop a Person — Dainfern Estate, 2021

June 2026 • 4 min read

Dainfern Estate spent R12 million on perimeter walls, electric fencing, and CCTV cameras. The intruder climbed a tree, dropped over the wall, and was inside for 11 minutes before anyone noticed. He was gone before the armed response vehicle arrived.

The fence worked. It detected the climb. But by the time the alert reached a human who could act, the intruder was already inside. EstateGuard wasn't designed to replace the fence. It was designed to close the gap between "something touched the perimeter" and "someone is inside the property" — by showing guards exactly where the person is, in real time, through walls.

🚢 MaritimeGuard

The Silent Deck — Safmarine SA Express, 2019

June 2026 • 4 min read

A crew member on the Safmarine SA Express was last seen on deck at 02:00. The next watch change at 04:00 noted his absence. By the time the ship turned back to search, 45 minutes had passed. The body was never recovered. The investigation concluded "presumed man overboard, time of incident unknown."

MaritimeGuard can't prevent a person falling overboard. But it can detect when it happens — a sudden absence at the rail, a person present one second and gone the next — and raise an alert within seconds, not minutes. In cold water, that difference matters.

🫁 Wellness+

He Died in His Sleep, Alone, And Nobody Knew — Protea Home, 2022

June 2026 • 4 min read

An elderly resident at a Protea Glen care home passed away in his sleep. Staff discovered him at the 06:00 check-in. He had been gone for six hours. The home had motion sensors, pressure mats, and emergency pull cords — none of which detected a person who simply stopped breathing.

Wellness+ uses the same WiFi CSI signal that powers our security products to detect breathing rate, heart rate, and sleep patterns. It's not a medical device. But it knows when someone stops breathing. And it alerts someone who can help.
