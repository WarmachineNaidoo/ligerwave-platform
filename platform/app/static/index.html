How It Works

No technician. No installation. No per-room sensors.

Plug in the router

Replace your existing WiFi router with our OpenWrt-powered device. It looks and works like a normal router.

Open the Ligerwave app, scan the QR on the box. The router auto-pairs to your account via API key exchange.

Technology

Phase-based CSI sensing

Different from every other approach in the market.

✔ Patent-protected architecture

We use phase coherence, not ACF

Most WiFi sensing systems (including Origin AI, acquired by ADT for $170M) use Auto-Correlation Function (ACF) analysis. This approach is covered by 225+ patents.

Ligerwave uses phase coherence peak tracking — analyzing how the phase of CSI subcarriers shifts as people move. This is a fundamentally different mathematical domain, giving us freedom to operate while achieving higher spatial resolution.

Key innovations include multi-zone path analysis (for pet vs. person discrimination), dual-band fusion (2.4 + 5 GHz for packet loss resilience), and breath-micro-motion extraction from stationary CSI frames.
